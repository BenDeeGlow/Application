My Motivation

Why Commerzbank?

I've been involved in blockchain since 2021 — starting with NFTs, which gave me an early, hands-on understanding of how these systems work in practice. Five years later, what was once considered speculative is now firmly on the agenda of regulators, central banks, and financial institutions worldwide.

What genuinely excites me isn't the technology in isolation — it's what it means for different parts of the bank. For corporate clients, tokenized assets and smart contracts could fundamentally change how financing and settlement work. For retail banking, programmable money opens up entirely new product possibilities. For risk and compliance teams, the transparency of distributed ledgers is both an opportunity and a challenge. And for IT and product teams, the infrastructure decisions being made right now will define how competitive European banks are in the next decade.

Why the IDDP?

I want to understand blockchain not just as a technology, but as a strategic question — one that touches every corner of the institution. A programme that rotates across four different business units feels like exactly the right place to develop that understanding. The question for banks like Commerzbank is no longer whether blockchain will reshape financial services — it's whether they'll be the ones shaping how that happens.

What I bring

Three years inside Commerzbank across Berlin, Zürich and Frankfurt have shown me how the institution works across retail, international corporates, and capital markets. I've seen how decisions get made, how teams coordinate across borders, and where digital tools are already changing the work — and where the gap still is. That inside perspective, combined with a genuine curiosity about what comes next, is what I'd bring to the IDDP.
