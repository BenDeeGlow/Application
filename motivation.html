My Motivation

Why Commerzbank?

In 2021 I started trading NFTs and Cryptocurrencies, thats why I initially came to Commerzbank. Because the dual study programme offered the fastest way into Banking and Finance whilst still being only 17 and getting a Degree

Why the IDDP?

The ability to rotate between different Units is something very unique. I want to use this ability to get the full 360° Perspective and learn how the bigger Picture presents itself

Why I'm a good fit

After three years in PSBC, CC and the IC-COO I built a base knowledge on how Commerzbank works and what our Clients need from us. Now I would like to build on this knowledge and shape the future
